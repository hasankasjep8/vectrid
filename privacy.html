Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Key point: Vectrid's core image vectorization runs entirely in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to our servers.

1. Overview

Vectrid ("we", "our", "us") provides a client-side image-to-SVG vectorization tool and an optional cloud API for developers. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

When you use the Vectrid web application at vectrid.vercel.app:

  • Images are not uploaded. All image processing happens locally in your browser using WebAssembly. Your images never leave your device.
  • No user accounts required. You can use the full web app without creating an account.
  • No cookies for tracking. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics.

3. Data We Collect

3.1 Local Storage

We store preferences (theme, presets, onboarding state) in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

3.2 API Usage (Developer API only)

If you use the Vectrid Developer API, we collect:

  • Account information: Email address and API key for authentication.
  • Usage data: Number of API calls, timestamps, and response sizes for billing and rate limiting.
  • Uploaded images: Images sent to the API are processed and immediately deleted after conversion. We do not store, cache, or analyze your images beyond the conversion process.

3.3 Hosting & Server Logs

Our hosting provider (Vercel) may collect standard server logs including IP addresses, browser type, and page requests. These logs are used for security and performance monitoring and are retained for up to 30 days.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the Vectrid service
  • To process API requests and manage billing
  • To monitor and prevent abuse
  • To communicate service updates (only if you opt in)

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only with:

  • Service providers: Hosting (Vercel), payment processing (Stripe) — only as needed to operate the service.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption for all data in transit, secure API key authentication, and automatic deletion of processed images.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Export your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Request information about how your data is processed

8. Children's Privacy

Vectrid is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at privacy@vectrid.com.
